PROCESSING (python / numpy / scipy; script kept outside the repo)
  1. Registration: each image is rotated by its published orientation and scaled by its published
     field of view, then translated so that field stars match heic1310a (least-squares similarity
     fit on matched stars: LBT 50 stars, rms 0.14″; MIRI 81 stars, rms 0.18″; NIRCam 31 matches).
     Fitted rotations/scales agree with the published values to < 0.15° and < 0.2 %, no mirror flip.
  2. Resampled (cubic, Gaussian pre-filter when downsampling) onto the common grid.
  3. Stars removed: compact peaks above a local median, saturated stars and their traced diffraction
     spikes masked, holes filled by multi-scale normalised convolution. The stars are re-drawn by
     the scene as point sprites with each telescope's PSF, at the catalogued positions.
  4. Black level subtracted (3rd percentile beyond 50″); low-frequency mismatch to the deep LBT halo
     removed near the frame edge; cross-faded into the tinted LBT halo over 46–62″.
  JPEG quality 94 (inner) / 92 (wide). Total ≈ 4.2 MB.

SCIENCE REFERENCES (geometry and numbers used by the 3D model)
  O'Dell, Ferland, Henney & Peimbert 2013, AJ 145, 92 — "Studies of NGC 6720 with calibrated HST/WFC3
    emission-line filter images I: structure and evolution" (arXiv:1301.6636): main ring = thick
    equatorial band (44″ x 30″ semi-axes, polar semi-axis ≈ 59″) with a central cavity and polar lobes
    pointing nearly at us; He II → [O III] → H I → [N II]/[S II] ionisation stratification; inner halo
    loops within ~65″, outer halo radius ≈ 115″; central star ≈ 120 000 K; knots at the ionisation front.
  Wesson et al. 2024, MNRAS 528, 3392 — "JWST observations of the Ring Nebula (NGC 6720): I. Imaging
    of the rings, globules, and arcs" (arXiv:2308.09027): ~20 000 globules, 0.2″ (≈150 au),
    n ≈ 10⁵–10⁶ cm⁻³; cavity radius ≈ 25″, shell outer radius 44″ x 35″; ~10 concentric arcs, spacing
    ≈ 1.5″ (orbit of a low-mass companion, P ≈ 280 yr); 300–400 radial "spikes" in the halo, ~0.4″
    wide, ~20″ long; distance 790 ± 30 pc; T_eff ≈ 135 000 K, L ≈ 310 L☉; age ≈ 4 000 yr.
  Illustration of the 3D structure: heic1310c, https://esahubble.org/images/heic1310c/
