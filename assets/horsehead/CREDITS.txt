Horsehead Nebula (Barnard 33 / IC 434) — data for js/scenes/nebulae/horsehead.js (scene "dark-nebula").

All textures here are DERIVED from the photographs below: each image was converted back to linear
radiance (inverse of the app's ACES + gamma 2.2 display transform), its stars were measured and removed,
and it was decomposed into
  *_b.jpg   emission behind the dust B (star-free image where not darkened, smooth fill behind the dust)
  *_l.jpg   the dust's own light L = I − B·exp(−τ), stored per unit of the depth profile (L·τ/(1−e^−τ))
  vis_*_d.jpg  R = sqrt(τ_V/10) dust optical depth, G = h/2 ly assumed half-thickness, B = ionised-gas
               fraction of L
  vis_w_gain.png  low-frequency colour gain matching the Hα mosaic to the ESO frame (log2-encoded)
  stars_*.bin   float32 [x, y, F_r, F_g, F_b, τ_V] per star: sky-plane position (ly), photometry
               (linear radiance × ly²) and the dust column at that position
Registration between the images: star matching (similarity / affine fits, 0.5–2.5 px rms) and, for
JWST, the footprint drawn in ESA/Webb's comparison graphic weic2411c refined with 33 matched stars.
Encodings: *_b texel^2.2 × scale, *_l texel^2.5 × scale (scales in horsehead.js, SC).
