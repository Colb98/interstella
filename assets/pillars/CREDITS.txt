Studied for reference only (not shipped): heic1501b (Hubble 2014 WFC3/IR), opo9544a (Hubble 1995 WFPC2,
J. Hester & P. Scowen), weic2216a (Webb, cropped), weic2218a (Webb MIRI, NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI,
J. DePasquale, A. Pagan), and McLeod et al. 2015, MNRAS 450, 1057, "The Pillars of Creation revisited
with MUSE" (arXiv:1504.03323) for the 3D geometry (§4.2, Fig. 21), photo-evaporation flow (~8 km/s,
vrel −3.4 … −7.1 km/s for P1a/P1b/P2/P3), mass-loss rate (70 M☉/Myr) and lifetime (~3 Myr).

FILES (all derived from 1 and 2; total ≈ 6.6 MB)
  hst.webp        2048 x 2136  Hubble mosaic, Lanczos-downsampled (0.1327"/px = 0.00367 ly/px), stars removed
  jwst.webp       2048 x 2136  NIRCam mosaic resampled onto the same grid (published WCS + a 2.7"/2.9" star-matched

                               the NIRCam mosaic does not cover are filled by pyramid inpainting

                               0.3 ly (empty-space skipping)

PROCESSING (pipeline/ — Python 3 + numpy, scipy, Pillow; run in this order on the downloaded JPEGs)
  s1_register.py   downsample Hubble to 2048 px; resample NIRCam onto that grid from the published WCS
  s2_stars.py hst  multi-scale star detection (ring test, spike-aware), radial-halo subtraction,
                   spike/core masks, push-pull inpainting + grain; removes knots on ionisation fronts
  s3_matte.py, s3b_mask.py, s4_geo.py
                   dust matte from background-normalised log(R/B) colour of both releases + darkness,
                   hand-traced pillar outlines (polys.py) with ragged multi-scale edges, per-structure depth
                   zc from McLeod et al. 2015 (P1a −2.0 → −1.0 ly tip toward us; base cloud +0.6; P1b +1.0 → +0.2;
                   P2 +2.0 → +0.6; P3 +1.8 → +2.4; veil/globules +1.4…+1.6), round cross-sections
  s2_stars.py jw   NIRCam stars (bright → removed, faint → kept; red stars inside dust = embedded protostars)
  s2b_shift.py     star-matched refinement of the NIRCam registration
  s5_export.py     background sheets, textures, geometry, per-star fits of the removed light
  s6_detail.py     native-resolution head crops

The depth model, slab thicknesses, the inpainted background behind the pillars, the dust colour on
the hidden sides and the positions of the NGC 6611 O stars (≈ 7.5 ly above the frame, towards
HD 168076) are modelled, not measured.
