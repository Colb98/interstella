Saturn data for the "Sao Thổ" scene (js/scenes/planets/saturn.js).
NASA mission data are public domain (U.S. Government works / NASA PDS); credit NASA/JPL-Caltech/Space Science Institute.

saturn_map_4k.jpg  4096x2048 sRGB JPEG q93, simple cylindrical, PLANETOGRAPHIC latitude (north up), east longitude
  0..360 deg left to right (System III).
  Source: Cassini ISS RGB global colour map of Saturn, "original" version, 11 Aug 2011 (RED/GRN/BL1 filters,
  3601x1801, 0.1 deg/pixel, ~161 km/pixel, phase 42.5 deg), NASA PDS Atmospheres Node, bundle co_iss_global-maps,
  doi:10.17189/rkkb-6y30
  https://atmos.nmsu.edu/PDS/data/PDS4/co_iss_global-maps/data_derived/Cassini_ISS_RGB_Saturn_global_color_map_original.fits
  Described in: Wang, X., Li, L., Jiang, X., West, R. A. (2025) "Multi-wavelength global maps of Jupiter and Saturn using Cassini imaging
  system data", Scientific Data 12, 45, doi:10.1038/s41597-025-04392-3.
  Processing: FITS rows run north->south (the header says otherwise; checked against the PDS browse image); the
  constant-filled column 0 was replaced by column 3600. Values treated as sRGB. Planetocentric -> planetographic
  (a=60268, c=54364 km). Gaps filled: the equator seam (+0.2..-0.6 deg, interpolated); the ring-shadowed band
  -1.8..-17.4 deg planetocentric (replaced by the mirrored +1.8..+17.4 deg band rolled 180 deg in longitude, with a
  linear zonal-mean correction so both edges join); beyond +78.8 / -81.9 deg the last rows' zonal mean (detail
  fading out); the scene replaces >76 deg N with saturn_npole.jpg and draws the south polar vortex procedurally.

saturn_zonal.f16  2048 x RGB float16 (little endian): zonal mean of saturn_map_4k.jpg in linear RGB, rows north->south.

saturn_npole.jpg  1024x1024 sRGB JPEG, north polar projection, pole at pixel (503, 509), 34.9 px per degree of
  colatitude (calibrated so the hexagon's circumradius is 14 500 km), edge ~75.5 deg N.
  Source: PIA17652 "In Full View: Saturn's Streaming Hexagon", Cassini ISS, 10 Dec 2012 (R = 750 nm, G = MT2 727 nm,
  B = BL1+VIO), NASA/JPL-Caltech/SSI/Hampton University.
  https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/psd/photojournal/pia/pia17/pia17652/PIA17652.tif
  Processing: brightness = the 750 nm channel (contrast x0.55); colour = the map's mean colour at 76-77 N,
  tinted blue-grey in proportion to the blue excess (small haze particles inside the hexagon) -- an approximation
  of true colour, the source is a false-colour composite.

saturn_rings.f16  16384 x 4 float16 (little endian), radius 73 000 .. 137 500 km (3.94 km per texel):
  channel 0 = normal optical depth tau, channels 1-3 = linear RGB particle reflectance (albedo x phase, relative).
  tau: Cassini Radio Science (RSS) X-band ring occultation, Rev 7 egress, 3 May 2005, 1 km resolution product
  RSS_2005_123_X43_E_TAU_01KM.TAB, PDS Ring-Moon Systems Node volume CORSS_8001 (E. Marouf et al.).
  https://pds-rings.seti.org/holdings/volumes/CORSS_8xxx/CORSS_8001/data/Rev007/Rev007E/Rev007E_RSS_2005_123_X43_E/
  Values above the per-sample threshold (~4-5, B-ring core) clamped to it; binned by averaging transmission
  exp(-tau); 0.004 noise floor removed; zero inside 74 350 km (the D ring is drawn procedurally). Note: X-band tau
  is slightly lower than visible-light tau in regions of small particles.
  colour: PIA11142 "A Full Sweep of Saturn's Rings", Cassini ISS natural colour mosaic, 26 Nov 2008, ~6-8 km/px.
  https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/psd/photojournal/pia/pia11/pia11142/PIA11142.tif
  Radial profile along the mosaic's symmetry row, pixel->radius from 7 anchors (C ring edge, Colombo gap, B ring
  edges, Encke, Keeler, A ring edge), outer-A frame gain matched across the Encke gap, divided by the
  single-scattering factor mu0/(mu0+mu)(1-exp(-tau(1/mu0+1/mu))) for sun 4 deg / view 10 deg, Gaussian-smoothed
  25 km, scaled so the B ring has luminance 0.62. Chromaticity from the sRGB-decoded mosaic, brightness from its raw
  values (the C/B ring contrast shows the raw values are close to linear).
