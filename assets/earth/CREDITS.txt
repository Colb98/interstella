earth_elev_4k.webp  4096x2048 lossless WebP
earth_himalaya_elev.webp  2048x1024 lossless WebP (same 1' crop as above)
  NOAA NCEI ETOPO 2022 Global Relief Model, 60 arc-second, ice surface + bathymetry, EGM2008 heights.
  https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/mgg/global/relief/ETOPO2022/data/60s/60s_surface_elev_gtif/ETOPO_2022_v1_60s_N90W180_surface.tif
  doi:10.25921/fd45-gt74. Area-averaged to 4096x2048 for the global map.
  Encoding: R,G = (elevation in metres + 11000) as a 16-bit big-endian integer (1 m steps on land,
  2 m on the shelf, 25 m below -250 m); B = water fraction (ocean from ETOPO < -50 m, lakes and
  coastlines from the Blue Marble ocean colour).

earth_night_8k.jpg  8192x4096 greyscale JPEG
  NASA Earth Observatory, Black Marble 2016 (Suomi NPP VIIRS day-night band), 3 km colour composite
  (BlackMarble_2016_3km.jpg, image record 144898), Joshua Stevens / Miguel Román / NASA GSFC.
  https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/144000/144898/BlackMarble_2016_3km.jpg
  The blue moonlit/airglow land background was removed (lights = luminance - min(0.66*B, 58)).

earth_storms.jpg  2048x1024 greyscale JPEG (4x2 cells of 512x512)
  Seven real storms from NASA GIBS (Global Imagery Browse Services) WMS, VIIRS Corrected
  Reflectance True Color (Suomi NPP / NOAA-20), public domain:
    cell 0  Hurricane Irma, 2017-09-05, centre 16.95N 59.0W, half-size 8 deg   (VIIRS_SNPP)
    cell 1  Hurricane Florence, 2018-09-11, 27.5N 66.3W, 8 deg                 (VIIRS_SNPP)
    cell 2  North Pacific cyclone, 2022-02-01/02, 48.2N 174.6W, 13 deg        (VIIRS_SNPP)
    cell 3  Southern Indian Ocean cyclone, 2026-10-16, 51.0S 109.6E, 13 deg   (VIIRS_NOAA20)
    cell 4  Developing West-Pacific tropical storm, 2026-10-16, 11.0N 152.0E, 8 deg (VIIRS_NOAA20)
    cell 5  Southeast Pacific cut-off low off Chile, 2026-10-16, 42.6S 92.5W, 11 deg (VIIRS_NOAA20)
    cell 6  North Atlantic cyclone, 2023-03-10, 50.3N 33.9W, 11 deg          (VIIRS_SNPP)
  Each cell is storm-centred azimuthal-equidistant (north up, x east), half-size as listed.
  Cloud brightness = true-colour luminance above the clear ocean / Blue Marble land surface,
  whiteness-weighted, tone-matched to the Blue Marble cloud composite (0.84 * v^1.5).
  https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/wms/epsg4326/best/wms.cgi
