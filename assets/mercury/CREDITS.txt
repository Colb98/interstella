Mercury data for the "Sao Thủy" scene (js/scenes/planets/mercury.js).
All source products are NASA / USGS data in the public domain (U.S. Government works; MESSENGER
data courtesy NASA / Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory / Carnegie Institution of
Washington, distributed by the PDS Imaging Node and USGS Astrogeology Science Center).
Map projection of every file: equirectangular (simple cylindrical), planetocentric latitude,
east-positive longitude -180..+180 (left to right), north up, sphere R = 2439.4 km.

mercury_lum_4k.jpg  4096x2048 greyscale JPEG q84
  Luminance (0.6 x 750 nm + 0.4 x 430 nm) of the MESSENGER MDIS 3-colour basemap ("MD3",
  R/G/B = 1000/750/430 nm), 64 pixels/degree (665 m/px), PDS data set MESS-H-MDIS-5-RDR-MD3-V1.0:
  https://asc-pds-services.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/mosaic/Mercury_MESSENGER_MDIS_Basemap_MD3Color_Mosaic_Global_665m.tif
  (USGS Astrogeology: "Mercury MESSENGER MDIS Basemap MD3 Color Global Mosaic 665m").
  Polar no-data pixels filled with the mean of their latitude row. Area-averaged (box) to 4096x2048.

mercury_md3_1k.jpg  1024x512 RGB JPEG q92
  The same MD3 3-colour basemap, area-averaged; used only for the (desaturated) natural colour.

mercury_enh_2k.jpg  2048x1024 RGB JPEG q90
  MESSENGER MDIS enhanced-colour basemap (R = PC2, G = PC1, B = 430/1000 nm ratio), 665 m/px:
  https://asc-pds-services.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/mosaic/Mercury_MESSENGER_MDIS_Basemap_EnhancedColor_Mosaic_Global_665m.tif
  (USGS Astrogeology: "Mercury MESSENGER MDIS Basemap Enhanced Color Global Mosaic 665m").

mercury_dem_2k.webp  2048x1024 lossless WebP     (DEM base)
mercury_dem_detail_4k.jpg  4096x2048 greyscale JPEG q88   (DEM detail)
  USGS MESSENGER global DEM, 665 m/px, v2 (Becker et al. 2016, "First global digital elevation model
  of Mercury", LPSC 47, #2959), heights relative to the 2440 km sphere:
  https://asc-pds-services.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/mosaic/Mercury_Messenger_USGS_DEM_Global_665m_v2.tif
  Rolled from 0..360 to -180..+180 E, area-averaged to 4096x2048 (3.7 km/px). Stored in two parts to
  fit the size budget:
    base:   2048x1024 area average, R,G = round((metres + 12000) / 8) as a 16-bit big-endian integer.
    detail: 4096x2048, value = 128 + round((DEM - bilinear upsample of base) / 6 m), clamped 0..255.
  Reconstruction: DEM = bilinear(base) + (detail - 128) * 6 m  (rms error 15 m).

Ephemeris and rotation used by the scene (no data files):
  JPL "Keplerian Elements for Approximate Positions of the Major Planets" (E. M. Standish),
  https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/approx_pos.html ; IAU WGCCRE 2015 rotation model for Mercury
  (Archinal et al. 2018, Celest. Mech. Dyn. Astron. 130:22). Phase curve: Mallama, Wang & Howard 2002,
  Icarus 155, 253.

Reference images used only for visual comparison (not shipped): NASA/JHUAPL/CIW PIA12371,
PIA16853, PIA17280.
